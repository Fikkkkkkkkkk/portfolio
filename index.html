How I build an app, from idea to release.

  1. Define the problem

    I understand who the app is for, what they need, and what a useful first version should solve.

  2. Plan the experience

    I map the main user flow, choose the right tools, and break the work into practical features.

  3. Build the core

    I develop the interface, application logic, and data flow around the app's most important task.

  4. Test and refine

    I check the app on real screens, fix weak points, and improve its clarity, speed, and reliability.

  5. Release and learn

    I publish a working version, collect feedback, and use what I learn to guide the next iteration.
